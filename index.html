SF Studio – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026

SF Studio is a Chrome extension designed for Salesforce developers to connect to authorized Salesforce organizations and explore or edit Apex Classes, Apex Triggers, and Lightning Web Components.

1. Information We Access

SF Studio may access Salesforce Instance URLs, Access Tokens, API versions, Salesforce development resources, and extension settings required to provide its functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Information is used to establish Salesforce connections, communicate with Salesforce APIs, retrieve development resources, and support the extension's features.

3. Access Token Security

Users provide their Salesforce credentials to connect to their organizations. Credentials are handled by the extension to provide its functionality. Users should keep their access tokens confidential.

4. Data Storage

Extension storage may be used for necessary settings and preferences. Data handling depends on the features used and the browser environment.

5. Data Sharing

SF Studio does not sell personal information or share it for advertising purposes. Salesforce information is used to communicate with services authorized by the user.

6. Third-Party Services

SF Studio communicates with Salesforce APIs. Salesforce services are subject to Salesforce's own privacy policies and terms.

7. User Control

Users choose which Salesforce organization to connect to and must have appropriate access permissions. Users can end their connection by closing or refreshing the extension.

8. Security

Users are responsible for protecting their Salesforce credentials and connecting only to authorized organizations.

9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated when the extension's features or data practices change.
